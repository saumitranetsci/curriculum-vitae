\begin{rSection}{Research Experience}
\begin{rSubsection}{\href{https://www.networkscienceinstitute.org/}{Network Science Institute}}{Sept 2024 -- Present}{Graduate Research Assistant}{Boston, USA}
\item Supervisor: Esteban Moro
\item \textbf{Grocery Accessibility and Shopping Behavior}: Developed a scalable \textbf{Python, SQL, and Snowflake} pipeline that transformed smartphone GPS data from \textbf{$\sim$20M devices} across 350 US metros into individual-level grocery-visitation panels.
\item Derived behavioral features beyond residential proximity by identifying individual activity anchors with \textbf{density-based spatial clustering} and learning user--brand preference embeddings through \textbf{non-negative matrix factorization}.
\item Built and evaluated \textbf{discrete choice models} of grocery-store selection, finding that activity-anchor proximity, brand preference, and store attractiveness were stronger determinants of choice than proximity to home; validated causally using \textbf{panel methods}.
\item \textbf{Modeling Business Dependency Networks}: Constructed behavior-based networks between businesses from co-visitation patterns, revealing relationships beyond geographic proximity.
\item Investigated whether \textbf{generative models of individual movement} reproduce these structures, then used \textbf{node2vec} to examine brand affinity as a driver of network formation.
\end{rSubsection}
